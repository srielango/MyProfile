About Me

I don't believe technology should be adopted simply because it is new or fashionable. It must solve a real problem, streamline an existing process, reduce unnecessary complexity, or create a tangible operational advantage.

With more than 24 years spent engineering, architecting, and modernizing software across diverse industries, I look beyond technology trends to understand how technical decisions directly impact day-to-day business operations. When technology is misaligned with real business needs, it creates technical debt, inflated cloud bills, and team frustration. When applied thoughtfully, it frees your team to focus on what matters.

My practice combines deep architectural experience with modern practical AI. Today, I use agentic workflows and AI coding tools (such as Claude Code and Gemini/Antigravity) to rapidly prototype ideas, automate complex data extraction, and evaluate technical feasibility in days rather than months.

As an independent advisor, I work primarily with business owners and founders who aren't technology specialists. I listen first, understand the operational reality, challenge assumptions, and help you determine whether technology is actually the right answer before you commit valuable time and capital.
